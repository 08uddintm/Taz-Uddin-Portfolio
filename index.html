About

Two worlds by eightThat’s why I’m at home in every room.

01

Where I’m from

I was born in the UK, but at three I moved to my family’s village, Isabpur, in Bangladesh.

I didn’t start formal education until I was eight, when I came back to London and started again: a new language, a new culture, and a classroom I’d never sat in before.

Two very different worlds, a village with very little and life in London, before I was ten. You learn quickly how to read a room, adapt to it, and find your place in it.

02

What it gave me

That’s the skill I’ve built a career on.

Today I move between engineers, commercial teams, C-suite and board-level discussions, and the other side of a negotiating table, and I can speak each one’s language. I’m not the deepest expert in any single room; I’m the person who connects them all.
